What Is FLOCO Certified?

Not a Franchise. A Certification Built Around Your Business.

FLOCO Certified is a selective network of established contractors who meet our standards for craftsmanship, customer service, and professionalism.

As a FLOCO Certified Specialist, you continue operating under your own company name while gaining access to the installation methods, branding, business resources, and support behind FLOCO.

There are no franchise fees or royalties. Your company remains yours, with the added advantage of an established rubber surfacing system and a recognized certification identity.

  • Your Business, Your Brand

    Keep your company name and independence while adding the FLOCO Certified designation.

  • One Specialist Per Market

    FLOCO works with one approved certified company in each designated territory.

  • More Than Installation Training

    Access the methods, marketing resources, and operational tools behind the FLOCO business.

Exclusive Territory Model

One FLOCO Certified Specialist Per Market

FLOCO Certified is built around quality, not quantity.

We select one qualified contractor for each approved territory to represent the FLOCO Certified name.

Our territory-based model gives approved specialists the opportunity to establish FLOCO Certified within their local market while maintaining their own independently operated business.

With a limited number of territories available, each application is carefully reviewed to determine whether the business and region are the right fit.
