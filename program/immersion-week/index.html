Five days. One company. Real job sites.

A Different Approach to Rubber Surfacing Training

Real Job Sites. Real Experience. Your Team.

FLOCO Immersion Week isn't a traditional classroom course.

We work with one company at a time, bringing your team directly into our day-to-day operations. You'll learn alongside the people who developed the FLOCO system, gaining experience with real installations, production challenges, and the details that make quality rubber surfacing work stand out.

There are no crowded seminars or generic demonstrations. The focus is on helping your team understand FLOCO's approach through practical, hands-on experience.

  • One Company at a Time

    Focused instruction built around your team, without competing for attention in a large training group.

  • Hands-On Job Site Experience

    Work directly with the FLOCO team on real rubber surfacing projects.

  • Installation and Business Knowledge

    Understand both the technical methods and the operational systems behind FLOCO.

The Five-Day Training Experience

What You'll Learn During FLOCO Immersion Week

Each day focuses on a different part of the FLOCO system, from the fundamentals of a quality installation to advanced design, problem-solving, and running a successful rubber surfacing operation.

  1. MonThe Install System
  2. TueAdvanced Prep
  3. WedLuxury Design
  4. ThuProduction & Problem-Solving
  5. FriThe Business of FLOCO
Monday · The Install System
01

Day 1: Learn the FLOCO Installation System

Get hands-on experience with the foundation of FLOCO's installation process.

Work alongside the team on a real rubber surfacing project and learn the methods used to create consistent, professional finishes.

Key learning areas
  • FLOCO installation fundamentals
  • Real-world installation workflow
  • Working alongside an experienced installation team
  • The details that contribute to a quality finished surface
Tuesday · Advanced Prep
02

Day 2: Advanced Surface Preparation

Quality rubber surfacing starts long before the final layer is installed.

Learn FLOCO's approach to surface preparation and how proper preparation and drainage corrections contribute to a durable, professional installation.

Key learning areas
  • Surface preparation
  • Drainage considerations and corrections
  • Understanding site conditions
  • Preparation methods that support long-lasting results
Wednesday · Luxury Design
03

Day 3: Custom Designs and Luxury Finishes

Explore the design possibilities that help distinguish FLOCO from standard rubber surfacing installations.

Learn how the team creates custom colour combinations, decorative inlays, and premium finishing details.

Key learning areas
  • Custom colour blends
  • Decorative inlays
  • Luxury rubber surfacing design
  • FLOCO finishing techniques
Thursday · Production & Problem-Solving
04

Day 4: Production and Real-World Problem-Solving

Every project presents its own challenges.

Learn how the FLOCO team approaches large surfaces, detailed edges, drainage issues, and installation challenges encountered on actual job sites.

Key learning areas
  • Managing larger installation areas
  • Edge details and finishing
  • Drainage challenges
  • Practical on-site problem-solving
Friday · The Business of FLOCO
05

Day 5: The Business Behind FLOCO Certified

The final day shifts from installation techniques to the systems that support the FLOCO business.

Spend time with Lexi and Brett learning how FLOCO approaches marketing, sales, operations, its cleaning and maintenance division, and the design studio.

Key learning areas
  • Marketing and lead generation
  • Sales processes
  • Business operations
  • Cleaning and maintenance services
  • FLOCO Design Studio
